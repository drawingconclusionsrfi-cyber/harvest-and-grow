Our Story

Roots Run Deep

Harvest & Grow started as a small neighborhood stand, pressing juice from whatever local farmers had in season. We've grown, but the mission hasn't changed: keep it simple, keep it real, and keep it close to the ground.

Every batch is cold-pressed on-site. Every ingredient is traceable. Every cup is a handshake between you and the land that grew it.

  • Whole ingredients over additives, always.
  • Local partnerships over long supply chains.
  • Reusable, low-waste packaging wherever possible.
  • A living wage for every person on our team.
